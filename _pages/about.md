Shanglin is a doctoral researcher with \href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jplQac8AAAAJ}{Klaus-Robert Müller} at the Technical University of Berlin and a Research Associate with \href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lc7OkdAAAAAJ}{Mitsuo Kawato} at ATR Japan. With over three years of experience in machine learning, Shanglin is dedicated to developing modern machine learning techniques for neuroimaging and quantum chemistry, while keeping interests in generic
machine learning topics. Shanglin actively collaborates with world-leading labs in machine learning, neural engineering, and molecular dynamics. His work has also been recognized by industry partners.
